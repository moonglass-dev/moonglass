# W7-6e sets the update channel

`docs/specs/update.md` (the opening paragraph and §1 "Polling", item 31 of §10) and `docs/specs/protocol.md` "Heartbeat", made final by FR5-2, say that W7-6e sets `Heartbeat.update_channel` from `update.channel` in agent.toml: `stable` reports `STABLE`, `canary` reports `CANARY`, and an absent key or an absent file reports `STABLE`. The W7-6e line in `docs/plan/build-plan.md` does not mention the field. It does not depend on W2-10b, the ticket that loads agent.toml in `agent connect` with `policy.LoadFile`. Its paths also leave out `cmd/moonglass/`, where that one loaded `policy.Config` has to be passed into `internal/agent` (`ConnectOptions` in `internal/agent/connect.go` carries no policy or channel today). After this ticket, the W7-6e line names the field and its source, waits for W2-10b, and can reach `cmd/moonglass/`. W7-6e then uses the single local-policy load (ADR-0004 decision 5) and does not read agent.toml a second way (R-6 finding 3).

## Scope

- `docs/plan/build-plan.md`, the `**W7-6e**` ticket line only:
  - Intent: add a clause saying that the agent sets `Heartbeat.update_channel` from `update.channel` in the agent.toml that `agent connect` loaded with `policy.LoadFile`, passed from cmd/moonglass into internal/agent, with the mapping of `docs/specs/update.md` §1 (`stable` → `STABLE`, `canary` → `CANARY`, absent key or absent file → `STABLE`).
  - `depends_on`: `W7-6a, W7-6b, W7-6d, W2-10b`. The existing three stay in their order, and W2-10b is appended.
  - `paths`: add `cmd/moonglass/` after `internal/agent/`. Every other path stays.
  - `risk: high` and the rest of the intent stay unchanged.
- No other line of the build plan changes. No spec, code or test changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- The W7-6e line names `Heartbeat.update_channel`, `update.channel` in agent.toml and `policy.LoadFile`, and gives the same three-case mapping as `docs/specs/update.md` §1 and `docs/specs/protocol.md` "Heartbeat", word for word in its values.
- The W7-6e line's `depends_on` is exactly `W7-6a, W7-6b, W7-6d, W2-10b`.
- The W7-6e line's `paths` are exactly `internal/update/, internal/agent/, cmd/moonglass/, deploy/systemd/, scripts/install, internal/installtest/, docs/specs/install.md`.
- The line still has the format of build plan §3 and still parses: `scripts/ticket-status` lists W7-6e with the new dependency, and no dependency cycle results (W2-10b does not depend on any W7 ticket).
- `git diff --stat main` touches only `docs/plan/build-plan.md` and this ticket's own docs, and the build-plan diff changes only the W7-6e line.
- `make ci` is green.

## Out of scope

- Building any of it: setting the field, passing the policy and its tests are W7-6e.
- The `--policy` flag and the agent.toml load in `agent connect`: W2-10b.
- The mapping itself and the three specs: decided and written by FR5-2.
- The other R-6 findings: FR6-1, FR6-2 and FR6-4 to FR6-8.

## Security notes

None in the change itself: it is docs only. It keeps `docs/threat-model.md` §8 and ADR-0004 decision 5 true for W7-6e. agent.toml is local policy that the control plane cannot change, and it is loaded once, by `policy.LoadFile` in `agent connect`. W7-6e must take the channel from that load and never read the file a second way.
