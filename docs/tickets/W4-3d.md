# Error-rate trigger

`deploy.Proxy` reads `GET /moonglass/counters` every 10 seconds and keeps 2 minutes of samples, discarding them on a proxy restart; `internal/rollback` computes the window, the previous release's rate at the switch and the trigger with integer arithmetic; shadow mode reports and keeps watching, enforced mode rolls back (`docs/specs/auto-rollback.md`, "Error rate")
